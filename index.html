Profile Review & Discussion

What actually happens in the session?

Your profile, seen through the panel's eyes

Most candidates know their own story well. What they don't know is how a stranger sitting across a panel table reads it — and that gap is exactly where admissions decisions are won or lost.

This session is a structured, honest conversation about who you are, what you've done, and where you're headed. We go through your academic background, your work experience, your extracurriculars, and the choices you've made — and together we figure out the clearest, most compelling way to present all of it.

There are no templates here. Your profile is yours. The goal is to help you articulate it with confidence, not to fit you into someone else's mould.

Your Background Academic record, work experience, and the narrative thread that connects it all
Your Goals Why MBA, why now, and why this school — made specific and honest, not generic
Your Gaps Low scores, career switches, gaps in experience — how to address them, not hide them
Your Differentiator What makes you stand out in a room of high-achieving applicants with similar profiles
What We Cover
Part 1
Profile walkthrough
A full read of your resume, academics, and work history — understanding what a panel sees before you've said a word.
Part 2
Story and narrative
Finding the thread that connects your past to your future — making your "why MBA" feel specific and genuinely yours.
Part 3
School fit and selection
Which schools suit your profile and goals — and how to tailor your positioning for each without sounding scripted.
Come with your resume and a rough sense of your target schools. Everything else — we'll figure out together.

Real pressure. Real feedback.

A mock interview is only useful if it feels like the real thing. This isn't a friendly chat — it's a structured simulation designed to put you in the same position you'll be in on the actual day, so nothing catches you off guard.

The session covers your professional background and past domain in depth, your preferred area of management and why it suits you specifically, your personal profile and the story behind your choices, and classic HR and behavioural questions that most candidates underestimate.

After the simulation, we debrief in full — what came across well, what didn't land, where you hesitated, and what to work on before the actual interview. Feedback covers both content and delivery.

Past Domain Your work experience, technical knowledge, and what you've genuinely learned on the job
Preferred Domain Why operations, consulting, finance, or marketing — and why it's the right next step for you specifically
HR Questions Strengths, failures, leadership moments, and the classic situational questions that trip people up
Full Debrief Verbal and non-verbal feedback — content, tone, body language, pacing, and a clear action plan

Pre B-School Chat

You got in. Now what?

Make the most of every single day

You've put in months — maybe years — of effort to earn your seat at your dream B-School. The hard part is done. But the two years ahead of you are some of the most defining of your career, and how you navigate them matters enormously.

The Pre B-School Chat is a relaxed, one-on-one conversation designed to help you walk in with a plan — not just excitement. Together, we'll map out a personalised strategy for your crucial first 100 days: which clubs to consider, how placements really work, what to prioritise, and how to build a network that lasts well beyond campus.

Think of it as a calm, informed conversation with someone who's recently been exactly where you're about to go.

First 30 Days Orientation, reading the room, clubs and committees to watch
Days 31 to 60 Academics, early internship signals, and building your core circle
Days 61 to 100 Summer placement prep begins — what most people miss at this stage
Your Specific Goals Operations, consulting, finance — your domain shapes your entire roadmap
Your 100-Day Roadmap
Day 1–30
Settle in and read the room
Orientation, club fairs, professor relationships, and figuring out the informal power structures that no brochure mentions.
Day 31–60
Build your foundation
Academic rhythm, committee commitments, and the earliest signals that matter for lateral and summer placements.
Day 61–100
Start playing the long game
Summer placement prep, alumni connects, and locking in the positioning that defines your MBA story for recruiters.
This is a personalised conversation, not a generic checklist. Every school and every goal is different — your roadmap will be too.
